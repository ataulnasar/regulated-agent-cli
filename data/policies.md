# Advisory Policy (fictional)

Simplified rules for the regulated-agent-cli demo. Modelled loosely on MiFID II suitability
requirements. Not real policy, not financial advice.

## Suitability {#suitability}

A product's risk level must not exceed the customer's risk tolerance: high-risk products must not
be recommended to low-risk customers. A product's minimum investment horizon must not exceed the
customer's investment horizon.

## Human Review {#human-review}

Retirement-related investment recommendations require approval by a second advisor before they
are presented to the customer.

## Evidence {#evidence}

Every recommendation must cite at least one policy section or product source that was retrieved
during the assessment. Citations to sources that were not retrieved are invalid.

## Incomplete Profile {#incomplete-profile}

If the customer's investment horizon or risk tolerance is missing, do not recommend a product.
Gather the missing information first and escalate to an advisor.

## Prohibited Recommendations {#prohibited}

Never promise or imply guaranteed returns. Complex products, such as leveraged trackers, must not
be recommended to customers under 25 or to customers without high risk tolerance.

## Four Eyes {#four-eyes}

The advisor who approves a recommendation must not be the advisor who requested the assessment.
